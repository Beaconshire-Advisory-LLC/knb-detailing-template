## What "owned outright" means

You own:

- The **GitHub repository** containing all the source code — created in your name, transferred to you on day one.
- The **Vercel account** that hosts the site — created in your name.
- The **domain** — registered in your name through Cloudflare.
- The **Supabase project** — your database, your customer data.
- The **Stripe account** — your money goes directly to your bank. We never touch it.
- The **Resend, Twilio, and Google Workspace accounts** — all yours.

Beaconshire Advisory **never holds credentials, never takes a cut of payments, never has access after launch**. We sit on the call with you, walk you through every step, and hand you the keys when we're done. If you ever want to take this somewhere else — to another developer, to no developer, to delete it — you can.

The footer of your site currently shows a small "Built by Beaconshire Advisory" credit. You can leave it as a courtesy or remove it — it's a single setting (we'll show you).

## What this costs to run after launch

Floor: **~$10 / month plus $10 / year** for the domain renewal.

Variable: Stripe takes 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction. Twilio bills about a penny per text message. Resend is free up to 3,000 emails per month.

You don't pay Beaconshire any subscription, ongoing fee, or revenue share. This is a one-time project.
