If you've never used a mobile detailer before, the first booking can feel uncertain. Where do we set up? Do we need water? What if it rains? Here's how we actually work.

## Setup

**Do you bring water?** Yes — we carry our own water in the truck, and we can hook to your spigot if you'd rather we use yours. Either works. Lakeside customers, we usually use your hose because it's right there.

**Do you bring power?** Yes — we have a quiet generator if you don't have an outdoor outlet, and we carry extension cords if you do. Generator runs about 60 dB at 10 feet, quieter than a residential AC unit.

**How much space do you need?** A standard parking spot per vehicle, plus a little extra for the rig. About 20 feet by 20 feet is comfortable for one car. Dockside boats just need a few feet of clear walking space along the boat.

**Driveway or garage?** Either. Garage is great for ceramic coatings because we want a controlled environment. For most washes, the driveway is fine.

**HOA rules?** This comes up a few times a year. Most HOAs are fine with mobile detailing as long as we're not making noise outside reasonable hours and we don't leave runoff on the street. We've never had a complaint.

## What we need from you

**Be home or leave a key/access.** Most customers stay home. Some leave the boat unlocked at the lift and we work without them there. Either works — just tell us in advance.

**Move other vehicles** if they're in the way. We need a clear approach.

**Tell us about quirks.** Pet hair, gate codes, tricky lift access, that one piece of hardware that's loose. Notes upfront save us time.

**Move stuff out of the vehicle interior** when we do a Full Detail. We'll work around your child seats and your bow saw, but if there's a layer of stuff on the floor we have to put it somewhere.

## Weather

**What if it rains?** Light rain — we'll usually still come for interior or covered work. Heavy weather, we'll reach out the day before to reschedule with no fee. You can also reschedule on your end up to 48 hours out for a full refund.

**Cold?** We work down to about 40°F outdoor for exterior services. Ceramic coatings need 50°F+ and humidity below 70%. Below that we'll suggest a garage or a different day.

**Wind?** Wind isn't a deal-breaker except for ceramic and paint correction, where dust contamination becomes a problem. We'll bring it up if relevant.

## Time

**How long does it take?** Express services run about an hour. Full Details run 3–5 hours depending on size and condition. Premium and ceramic are 6+ hours. We always give you a target window when we confirm the booking.

**Are you on time?** Yes, almost always. Lake-area traffic is forgiving and our service routes are tight. If we're going to be more than 10 minutes late, we'll text.

**Do I need to be home the whole time?** No — you can leave for lunch, do errands, work from home. We just need access at the start and end.

## Payment

**How much is the deposit?** 25% at booking, the balance after we finish. Both go through Stripe — you'll get a secure payment link by text.

**Do you accept cash?** Yes, but card is easier for both of us. We don't keep change on the truck.

**Tips?** Appreciated, never expected. Card tips work fine.

**Cancellation refund?** Full refund &gt;48 hours out, 50% refund 24–48 hours, no refund within 24 hours. We're flexible on weather and emergencies — call us.

## After the detail

**Do you take photos?** Yes — before and after on every Full Detail and above. They live in your customer portal. Some clients use them for insurance documentation.

**What if I find a problem after you leave?** Call us. We'll come back. Real businesses don't leave problems unfixed.

**How often should I have you back?** Depends on the vehicle. A lakeside daily driver wants Express every month or Full every quarter. A garaged weekend Corvette can go 6 months between Full Details. We can recommend a cadence after we see how your vehicle holds up.
